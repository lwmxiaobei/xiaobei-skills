# Story formula

## Copy architecture

Write the persistent title as four beats:

1. Hook: a short emotion or surprise marker
2. Contrast: the subject and unexpected action
3. Mechanism: the tool, decision, or method
4. Payoff: the outcome or larger meaning

Use up to three yellow headline lines plus one white conclusion line. Prefer 8 to 18 Chinese characters per line. Avoid punctuation heavy prose in the headline.

Write the persistent summary as four logical blocks:

1. Prior state or problem
2. Trigger and decision
3. Method and evidence
4. Result and meaning

Keep 45 to 180 Chinese characters. Use seven to ten manually edited lines when possible. Each line should express one clause and fit without shrinking below 34 pixels at 1080×1920.

## Cover copy architecture

Make the cover understandable on its own. Write two to four centered `cover_lines` that cover these functions:

1. Subject or event
2. Important qualification, limitation, or contrast
3. Concrete result
4. Key number or larger meaning when available

Use an optional centered `cover_kicker` for source context, product state, or event category. Use zero to two centered `cover_evidence` lines for concise supporting facts such as review method, participant count, or source provenance.

Keep each main cover line to roughly 8 to 18 Chinese characters. Keep evidence lines shorter and visually subordinate. Do not use a generic phrase such as “重大突破” as the entire cover message. Do not hide a qualification that changes the meaning of the result.

## Evidence selection

Select evidence for narrative function, not visual variety alone:

1. Subject recognition
2. Primary source proof
3. Context or translation
4. Concrete outcome
5. Thematic close

For an article with no images, use quote cards containing exact short excerpts or faithful paraphrases labeled with the article source. Keep excerpts brief and respect copyright limits.

For a source video, extract frames that show different claims, actions, interfaces, products, or reactions. Avoid consecutive near duplicates. Preserve a frame with readable source identity when possible.

For screenshots, crop away empty browser chrome only when the source and meaning remain identifiable. Never crop out a qualification that changes the claim.

## Duration and pacing

Default reading speed is 5.5 Chinese characters per second. Add two seconds for orientation and switching attention between the headline, evidence, and summary.

Use 2.2 to 4 seconds per evidence card. The first human image may be shorter. Primary evidence and dense screenshots may be longer. Use a 0.12 to 0.18 second white flash only after the first one or two cards. Use direct cuts for the remaining proof sequence.

If the user specifies an exact duration, rewrite the summary and adjust evidence density before changing reading speed. Do not satisfy the target by adding silent holds.

## Factual and publication checks

1. Record the source URL, account, author, or publication in `source_label`.
2. Distinguish a source claim from a verified fact.
3. Do not infer income, motive, health, identity, or causation from visual material alone.
4. Treat emotional title language as framing, not permission to invent facts.
5. Verify music, screenshot, and image rights before commercial use.
6. Confirm that the cover remains accurate and understandable when viewed without the video or publish description.
